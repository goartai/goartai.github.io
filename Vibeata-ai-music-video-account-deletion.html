About Account Deletion

Vibeat - AI Music Video & MV is an AI-powered music and video creation application operated by UXOLCLUB TECHNOLOGY CO., LIMITED.

We respect your privacy and provide users with the ability to request permanent deletion of their Vibeat accounts and associated personal data.

You can submit an account deletion request even if you no longer have the Vibeat app installed on your device.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Vibeat account, please follow these steps:

  1. Click the Request Account Deletion button below.
  2. Send an email to Support@goartai.com using the subject Vibeat Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address associated with your Vibeat account, including the Google account email if you signed in using Google.
  4. Our support team may request additional verification to confirm account ownership before processing the deletion.
  5. Once your identity has been verified, we will process the deletion request and notify you when it is completed.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

When your verified account deletion request is processed, the following categories of data associated with your Vibeat account will be deleted or permanently de-identified, where applicable:

  • Account information: Account identifiers, profile information, and account-related personal information.
  • Uploaded content: Photos, audio files, videos, and other user-submitted materials stored with your account.
  • Generated content: AI-generated music, videos, lyrics, projects, and saved creations associated with your account.
  • Associated personal data: Personal information linked to your account that is no longer necessary and is not subject to a lawful retention requirement.

Account deletion is permanent. Deleted account information and content may not be recoverable.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain limited information may be retained when required or permitted for legitimate purposes, including:

  • Purchase, payment, and transaction records required for accounting, refunds, tax reporting, or legal compliance.
  • Security logs or records necessary for fraud prevention, abuse investigation, and protection of our services.
  • Information required to comply with applicable laws, legal obligations, or legitimate dispute resolution.

Any retained information will be limited to what is necessary for those purposes and kept only for the applicable retention period.

Residual copies in system backups may remain temporarily until they are removed under applicable backup retention schedules.

Subscriptions and Purchases

If you have an active subscription purchased through Google Play, deleting your Vibeat account does not automatically cancel that subscription.

Please manage or cancel your subscription through Google Play:

Manage Google Play Subscriptions

If you need assistance with an account deletion request involving a subscription, please contact our support team.

Processing Time and Confirmation

We review account deletion requests after receiving them and may contact you to verify account ownership.

We will process verified requests within the timeframe required by applicable law and notify you when deletion has been completed.

If certain information must be retained, we will provide relevant information about applicable exceptions where required.
